# Permission Slips

One domain of onto-magic: an example of what you can do when YCYOR. Requires `core/onto_magic_preamble.xml`, which holds beliefs, amplitude, Allowance, Field, Reflection, Creator and the ladder. None of that is restated here.

## The chain

Permission slips cannot be explained on their own. The order is beliefs, then the Art of Allowing, then YCYOR, then permission slips. The first three are core; this domain covers the fourth.

## What is settled

Permission slips are access cards to parts outside the usual allowance. Tools hold no power: they give access to power you already have. They are the UI; spirit is the backend. The teaching is the belief-to-form pipeline, not the objects. You are the source.

## Critical

The access card definition was given while "the Field" meant the set of beliefs. In the ontology the set of beliefs is the Allowance, and the Field is the surface reflected onto. What a permission slip gives access to must be restated in those terms before it is used in teaching or writing.
